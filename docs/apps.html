Getting more apps: the Software Manager

The Software Manager (the orange bag) is the app store, with thousands of apps from Flathub: Firefox, Chrome, Brave, GIMP, LibreOffice, Discord and many more.

  1. Open the Software Manager. Explore shows popular apps and categories, or type to search.
  2. Click an app, then Install. The first app also downloads shared parts (a few hundred MB), so it takes a while.
  3. The app appears in the start menu and gets a desktop shortcut.

Installed lists your apps with Open and Update all. Open an app's page to Remove it. Apps are kept on the USB stick, so they're still there after a restart.

Older apps and games (X11)

Apps that don't support Wayland (many games, Minecraft, older programs) open inside an "X11 apps" window. Alt + drag moves their windows. It closes by itself when the last one quits.
